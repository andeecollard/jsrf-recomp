5. "PLAYER SPEED IS WRONG" -- the 15:09 log has vsh_dp_zero on, so the DP4
   hypothesis from section 5 of the previous handover is DEAD. The tree
   already knows the mechanism (HANDOVER_..._SIXTY_FPS_AND_THE_PICTURE_IS_RIGHT
   line ~190, kernel_bridge.c BRIDGE_VBLANK_PERIOD_US): a late vblank poll
   DROPS the periods it missed, so game speed = 16.683 ms / mean frame. The
   player's session: [FRAME] mean 18.14 ms (92%), with [FRAME-WIN] windows at
   30-49 fps (50-80% speed). A jump in a 40 ms frame window plays at half
   speed: that is "floating". Two levers, one taken:
     - RECOMP_METAL_FF=1 added to paths.conf (23.53 -> 17.31 ms measured;
       jsrf_ff_msl_diff_test passed on this build, which is the stated
       precondition).
     - The flip sync (previous handover section 4) is still the largest
       remaining per-frame cost and is unchanged.
   A THIRD OPTION NOT TAKEN: deliver missed vblank periods instead of dropping
   them. kernel_bridge.c chose dropping deliberately ("one late vblank, not
   five at once") and the drop is what makes the frame rate the game speed.
   A catch-up mode capped at 2 periods would hold speed at the cost of
   stutter; it is a switch worth building and measuring, not a default.
